Who I am

About 🙂

Web developer, photographer, artist. Building from the Kenyan coast.

The short version

A curious human intrigued by how things work — and stubborn enough to build them from scratch.

I write HTML, CSS and JavaScript by hand, and back it with Node.js and PostgreSQL when a project needs real data behind it. Android when it needs to live in someone's pocket. No frameworks unless they earn their place.

Portrait and editorial photography, mostly black and white. Hard light, real faces, no over-retouching. The same eye goes into the interfaces — if it doesn't read at a glance, it isn't finished.
